# Mastercard — Business Structure, Segments, Org Chart, Product Taxonomy, M&A & Strategy (as of September 2026)

Research compiled to anchor a "map" of Mastercard's assets/capabilities. Sources cited inline; unconfirmed items flagged.

---

## 1. Financial reporting segments / revenue categories

Mastercard does **not** report multiple SEC/ASC 280 operating segments — like Visa, it discloses financials as effectively one payments business, but breaks net revenue into **two named categories** in its 10-K and earnings releases:

- **Payment network** — "revenue is primarily generated from charging fees to our customers based on GDV (Gross Dollar Volume, both domestic and cross-border) on the cards that carry our brands, and for providing switching and other network-related services." (i.e., the core "toll-booth" card-network economics)
- **Value-Added Services and Solutions (VAS&S)** — revenue from:
  - Security solutions
  - Consumer acquisition and engagement services
  - Business and market insights
  - Digital and authentication solutions
  - Processing and gateway
  - Other solutions (ACH batch and real-time account-based payments, bill payments, cross-border services, open finance)

FY2025 (per 4Q25 earnings release): total net revenue **$32.8B** (+16% y/y), of which Payment network net revenue grew 12% to roughly **$22.6B (~69%)** and Value-Added Services and Solutions grew 23% (21% ccy-neutral) to roughly **$10.2B (~31%)** — VAS&S continues to grow faster than the network business.
Source: [4Q25 Mastercard Earnings Release (PDF)](https://s25.q4cdn.com/479285134/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/4Q25-Mastercard-Earnings-Release.pdf); [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000013/ma-20251231.htm)

**Separately**, at its November 13, 2024 Investor/Investment Community Meeting, Mastercard framed its business (and total addressable market) around **three strategic priorities** — these map onto but are distinct from the revenue categories above, and are the same names used for the 2024 org restructure (see §2):

1. **Consumer Payments** (org unit name: **Core Payments**) — the secular shift from cash/checks to digital, ~$54T serviceable market.
2. **Commercial and New Payment Flows** — B2B/commercial: point-of-sale purchases (~$17T), invoiced payments (~$63T), disbursements & remittances via Mastercard Move (~$20T).
3. **Services and Other Solutions** (org unit name: **Services**) — ~$490B TAM; security, consumer acquisition/engagement, business & market insights, processing/authentication/gateway. VAS revenue ~17% CAGR 2022–2024 (ccy-neutral), ~85% recurring.

Source: [November 13, 2024 Mastercard Investment Community Presentation (PDF)](https://s25.q4cdn.com/479285134/files/doc_events/2024-Mastercard-Investment-Community-Presentation.pdf)

*Not confirmed verbatim:* the exact ASC 280 "single reportable segment" sentence was not pulled from the 10-K text; this is standard for card networks and consistent with all sources reviewed, but flagged as unconfirmed verbatim wording.

## 2. The 2024 organizational restructure (and 2026 update)

### April 2024 restructure (effective May 1, 2024)
Announced by CEO Michael Miebach as "Positioning Mastercard for the Next Era of Growth." Created **three new business units**, each reporting to Miebach:

| Unit | Original (2024) Leader | Title | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Payments** | Jorn Lambert | Chief Product Officer | Core payment products/platforms, real-time payments, acceptance innovation, consumer value propositions |
| **Commercial & New Payment Flows** | Raj Seshadri | Chief Commercial Payments Officer | Commercial cards (SME, T&E, purchasing, fleet, virtual), B2B AP/AR, non-card bill pay, remittances, disbursements, healthcare solutions |
| **Services** | Craig Vosburg | Chief Services Officer | Consolidated fraud, risk, cybersecurity, insights/analytics, loyalty; includes a newly formed **Data & AI** organization led by **Greg Ulrich, Chief AI and Data Officer** |

Ajay Bhalla (President, Cyber & Intelligence, 30+ year veteran) retired as part of this change.
Sources: [Mastercard press release, April 2024](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2024/april/positioning-mastercard-for-the-next-era-of-growth.html); [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/mastercard-to-realign-organisational-structure-with-three-new-divisions); [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/24/04/38181568/mastercard-revamps-structure-for-growth-new-teams-to-drive-core-payments-and-services)

### June 2026 leadership reshuffle (effective August 3, 2026)
Announced as "Mastercard reshapes leadership to accelerate global growth." The three-pillar structure (Core/Consumer Payments, Commercial & New Payment Flows, Services) persists, but leadership rotated and go-to-market was consolidated:

| Executive | Previous role | New role (Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ling Hai** | President, Asia Pacific, Europe, Middle East & Africa | **Chief Financial Officer** |
| **Sachin Mehra** | Chief Financial Officer | **Chief Business Officer** — new role unifying Country Operations, Sales Enablement, Global Partnerships, Digital Commercialization |
| **Linda Kirkpatrick** | President, Americas | **Chief Services Officer** (succeeds Vosburg) |
| **Dimitrios (Dimi) Dosis** | President, Eastern Europe, Middle East & Africa | **Chief Commercial Payments Officer** (succeeds Seshadri) — leads Commercial & New Payment Flows |
| **Jorn Lambert** | Chief Product Officer | Continues as **Chief Product Officer**, now explicitly covering stablecoin/agentic/core payments under a "Consumer Payments" framing |
| **Craig Vosburg** | Chief Services Officer | **Vice Chair** (global ambassador/senior stakeholder relations role) |
| **Raj Seshadri** | Chief Commercial Payments Officer | **Senior Strategic Advisor to the CEO** (key client engagement, emerging strategic areas) |
| **Tim Murphy** | Vice Chair | **Retiring** (October 2026) |

Framed as unifying customer focus into one integrated team and consolidating go-to-market leadership under Mehra.
Sources: [Mastercard press release, June 2026](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/june/mastercard-leadership-updates.html); [Mastercard Investor Relations news](https://investor.mastercard.com/investor-news/investor-news-details/2026/Mastercard-announces-leadership-updates-to-advance-strategy-and-support-continued-growth/default.aspx); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/personnel/2026/mastercard-names-new-cfo-amid-larger-leadership-reshuffle/)

## 3. mastercard.com/business — website product taxonomy

Mastercard's business-facing sites are split across a few properties: `mastercard.com/us/en/business` (main "For Business" hub — redirects from mastercard.us), and `b2b.mastercard.com` (a separate, more product-engineering-flavored B2B portal). Their menu structures differ somewhat; both are captured below.

### mastercard.com/us/en/business — "For Business" hub navigation
**Explore Solutions** (top-level categories):
- **Consumer payments**
- **Commercial payments**
- **Money movement**
- **Open finance**
- **Advisors & transformation**
- **Cybersecurity & fraud prevention**
- **Consumer acquisition & engagement**
- **Insights & intelligence**
- **AI**
- **Digital Asset Solutions**

**Explore by Industry Segment:** Financial institutions · Small and medium enterprises · Large corporations · Public sector · Fintech · Acquirers and PSPs · Healthcare

**Partner Programs:** Partner Advantage Program · Network Enablement Partners (NEP) · Mastercard Move

**Support:** Find a payment facilitator · Network Enablement Partners · Mastercard rules and compliance programs · BIN account range table

Source: [mastercard.com/us/en/business.html](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/business.html)

## 5. 2025–2026 strategic themes

- **Agentic commerce / Agent Pay** — Launched **April 29, 2025**. Introduces **Mastercard Agentic Tokens** (built on existing tokenization) so AI agents can transact on a user's behalf inside conversational/AI interfaces. Four pillars: Trusted Agent Registration, Safe Transactions, Consumer Control, Fraud Protection. Launch partners included Microsoft (Azure OpenAI/Copilot Studio), IBM (watsonx Orchestrate), Braintree, Checkout.com.
  Source: [Mastercard press release, April 2025](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2025/april/mastercard-unveils-agent-pay-pioneering-agentic-payments-technology-to-power-commerce-in-the-age-of-ai.html)

- **Agent Pay for Machines (AP4M)** — Launched **June 10, 2026**, extending Agent Pay to fully autonomous machine-to-machine/micro-transactions ("programmatic, always-on" payments). Four mechanisms: Credentialing, Permissioning, Transacting, Settling — paired with **Verifiable Intent** technology. 30+ launch partners incl. Stripe, Coinbase, Adyen, Solana Foundation.
  Source: [Mastercard press release, June 2026](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/june/mastercard-launches-agent-pay-for-machines.html); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/06/10/mastercard-prepares-for-a-future-where-ai-agents-make-payments-with-latest-introduction)

- **Mastercard One Credential** — A single digitally-connected credential letting consumers manage multiple funding sources (debit, credit, prepaid, installments) and payment preferences from one bank-issued credential/app.
  Source: [Mastercard One Credential page](https://www.mastercard.com/global/en/business/payments/consumer-payments/next-gen-payments/mastercard-one-credential.html); [Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/mastercard-launches-one-credential-to-give-gen-zers-access-to-multiple-payment-methods-via-a-single-card/)

- **Tokenizing all online transactions in Europe by 2030** — Announced **June 11, 2024**: Mastercard committed to phasing out manual 16-digit card entry for European e-commerce in favor of tokenization by 2030.
  Source: [Seeking Alpha summary](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4114749-mastercard-to-end-manual-card-entry-for-online-payments-in-europe-by-2030-report)

- **Mastercard Move** — Mastercard's money-movement/disbursements & remittances platform, cited in the 2024 Investor Day as addressing a ~$20T disbursements/remittances market; also appears as a named "Partner Program" in the current business-site navigation.
  Source: [Nov 2024 Investment Community Presentation](https://s25.q4cdn.com/479285134/files/doc_events/2024-Mastercard-Investment-Community-Presentation.pdf)

- **Multi-Token Network (MTN)** — Blockchain-based bank-token infrastructure for 24/7 tokenized interbank settlement and app-initiated payments; interoperating with J.P. Morgan's Kinexys (announced Nov 2024) and expanding into institutional asset tokenization (e.g., Ondo Finance joining MTN).
  Source: [Mastercard MTN page](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/business/payments/consumer-payments/next-gen-payments/digital-asset-solutions/multi-token-network.html); [Mastercard Newsroom, Ondo Finance](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/Insights/2025/making-institutional-financial-assets-available-in-digital-form-with-our-blockchain-technology.html)

- **Stablecoin strategy / BVNK acquisition** — Through 2025 Mastercard enabled stablecoins (USDG, PYUSD, USDC, FIUSD) on its network, then announced the **$1.8B acquisition of BVNK** (Mar 17, 2026, closed Aug 3, 2026) to connect on-chain payments with fiat rails, explicitly framed as making Mastercard "chain-agnostic."
  Source: [Mastercard stablecoin utility story](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/stories/2025/mastercard-stablecoin-utility-and-scale.html); [BVNK press release](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/march/Mastercard-to-acquire-BVNK-to-connect-on-chain-payments-and-fiat-rails.html)

- **"Services" as a growth engine** — Repeated management messaging (earnings calls, Investor Day) that Value-Added Services & Solutions is Mastercard's fastest-growing, highest-margin, most-recurring (~85% recurring) revenue pool, now ~31% of net revenue and growing faster (+23% in 2025) than the core Payment Network business (+12% in 2025).
  Source: [4Q25 Earnings Release](https://s25.q4cdn.com/479285134/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/4Q25-Mastercard-Earnings-Release.pdf); [Fintech Wrap Up deep dive](https://www.fintechwrapup.com/p/deep-dive-mastercards-value-added)
